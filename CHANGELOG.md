## Unreleased

- BusyBox lane ([record](docs/implementation.md#busybox-lane)): the host's unmodified static `/usr/bin/busybox` (v1.36.1, sha256 `1097cdef...f0d`, never committed) runs on the kernel and matches the pinned Linux 6.18.57 line for line. Two new oracle groups, `names` (338 lines, TCG `qemu64`) and `busybox` (308 lines, KVM; `unavailable` without the program, KVM or AVX-512), recorded in `target/linux-reference/init-reference-016` and `-017`; a generated second initramfs module (`busybox.cpio`). Fixes found on the way: `path_init` returns `ENOTDIR` for a non-directory `dirfd` with a relative path, initramfs symlinks get the archive time. Fix round 1 ([record](docs/implementation.md#busybox-lane-fix-round-1)): `AT_EMPTY_PATH` with a pipe descriptor stops loudly instead of acting on the working directory; `getrandom` clamps to `MAX_RW_COUNT` before `access_ok`; `reboot` answers `EINVAL` for `SW_SUSPEND` and `KEXEC` as the reference configuration does (new rows `reboot-*`, `mknodat-*`); a `vfork` parent with an armed `ITIMER_REAL` no longer hangs the kernel; a nested `vfork` whose middle task is killed forwards the address space to the lender; `chown` honours `setattr_prepare`'s `ATTR_MODE` check; the bare-metal `xstate` group is `unavailable` on a CPU with AMX. Not done: procfs applets, large `execve` argument blocks (more than four pages answer `E2BIG`), the `setuid` family, `RENAME_EXCHANGE`, `/dev/random`. Fix round 2: a `vfork` chain of three with both middle tasks killed no longer relocks `VFORK_FORWARD` (new `names.vfork-chain-*` rows), and `faccessat2`/`readlinkat` stop loudly on a pipe with an empty path.
